“when you design interfaces you are —literally— designing the sensory organs people use to perceive information”

— É. Urcades, via Inside the digital sensorium

This is truly how I think about design. If an interface is how someone senses the world, then every colour, word, and pixel changes what they notice, and what they miss.

I want people to feel that way about the things I create. I make things because I’m curious: a colour tool inspired by an 18th-century instrument for measuring the sky, a button interaction based on a painting, and a website where strangers leave confessions.

I’m excited by how new technology affects culture and by new ways of sharing and learning information.
