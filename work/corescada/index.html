CoreSCADA

GeologicAI, 2026

CoreSCADA is the software that runs GeologicAI’s core scanner: starting scans, checking every image before it is released, and keeping the instruments in order.

I design every interface at GeologicAI, from the hardware that scans core to CoreTable and AI products for mineral discovery. This walkthrough was recorded against the scanner simulator.

Home, the sidebar, notifications, light and dark themes, language and the account menu. The sidebar collapses out of the way.

Starting a scan is a short wizard: project, geo source, plan and box details, then start. Each stroke’s progress shows live, and the scan pauses before the wet photo stroke.

Every image from a scan is marked pass or fail before release. Filter by stroke or instrument, decide cards one at a time or together, go through them one by one in Focus review, then complete QC and let the scan finish.

The scan log: search, filter by project and date, sort, choose columns and page through.

Logs: filter by type, open an entry’s details, search, filter by date, sort and refresh.

Maintenance: set up the reference scan, see the last result, and work through a safety checklist that unlocks Start Reference Scan.

Instruments: each one’s connection and live state, with Clear and Abort.

Projects, scans, instruments and images are demo data.
