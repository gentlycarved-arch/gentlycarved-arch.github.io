CoreTable

GeologicAI, 2026

CoreTable is where geologists log drill core. This is a walkthrough of the prototype, from opening a workspace to signing off a log with teammates.

I design every interface at GeologicAI, from the hardware that scans core to CoreTable and AI products for mineral discovery. These recordings come from the working prototype, with demo data.

Logging happens on the Striplog. Select an interval and describe it, draw a new one, let Smart Fill take care of the repetition, and undo anything.

Shortcuts for people who log all day: Shift to snap, Alt to resize, double-click to fill a gap, right-click to split.

Intervals can also be drawn and adjusted straight on the core photos.

Add and hide tracks, blend overlays over the photos, add a curve and set its range.

Apply the project’s viewer template, change it, save your own, and switch back.

View mode is read-only, with hover cards for the details and a switch to turn them off.

Depth correction: measure each tube of core, split with Shift, mark rubble with Alt, and check every piece.

Place the driller’s depth blocks, with live checks, legend tips and a visibility toggle on every layer.

Everything saves as you go. A finished log is signed off, and every version can be looked back on and restored.

See who else is in the log, follow them, accept a teammate’s new depths, and toggle their outlines.
