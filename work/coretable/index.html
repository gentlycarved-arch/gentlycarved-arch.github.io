CoreTable

GeologicAI, founding designer, 2026

Core Table is where geologists turn drill core into data. It is the core logging area of GeologicAI’s AMP platform.

Geologists read high-resolution photos of core trays and a striplog of the hole, measure and correct depths, and log intervals of lithology, alteration and mineralization, often several people on the same hole and under client deadlines.

I worked as the product designer alongside the engineering team, in two parts. The first was my own clickable prototype, built to AMP’s design system, which brought the existing Core Table tools into one workspace: Core Photo and Striplog viewers that open from the Launcher and sit side by side, a clear View, Log and Depth correct mode switch, viewer templates, autosave with sign-off and version history, and teammate presence.

The second was layered on the engineering team’s own logging prototype. There I added multi-user logging, with no locking, live edits, a deliberately vague “who is updating what” note, and each teammate’s selection and working area shown in their colour. I also added a consistent set of indications that make every logging shortcut show what it will do before it happens.

The thread running through the work is reducing friction for expert users who work fast: make the system’s intent visible, keep people aware of each other without interrupting them, and avoid the confirmation prompts and locks that slow loggers down. Each idea was prototyped in working HTML and recorded as a short, focused demo so the team could review interactions one at a time.

Select, describe, draw and fill intervals on the striplog, with autosave and undo. Logging becomes a direct, visual task instead of form entry.

Shift snaps, Alt resizes, double-click fills and right-click splits, each with a hint line before it acts. Expert shortcuts become discoverable and predictable.

Log directly on the core photo rows, in the same tracks as the striplog. Geologists can log what they see on the rock, where they see it.

Add or hide tracks, blend overlays and style curves from one panel. Each viewer shows just the data that matters for the task.

Apply a project template, change it, save your own and switch back. Teams get consistent layouts and individuals keep their own setups.

A read-only View mode with optional hover cards. Reviewers can read the log without any risk of changing it.

Switch to Editing, then measure a tube in one click, split at fractures and mark rubble. Depth correction is faster and harder to do by accident.

Place driller’s depth blocks, see live checks for problems, and control layers from the legend. Errors surface while you work, not in a report afterwards.

Changes save automatically, sign-off records who checked the hole, and versions can be restored. Work is never lost and approvals are on record.

See who is in the hole, follow them, and accept a teammate’s new depths when ready. People share a hole without their depth scale moving under them.

All videos use dummy data. These are design proposals and prototypes, not shipped features. The engineering logging prototype that the multi-user work builds on was made by the engineering team.
